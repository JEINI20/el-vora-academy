ABOUT EL-VORA ACADEMY

Learn. Speak. Grow.

EL-VORA Academy is a welcoming online learning space for people who want to improve their Hindi or English from the basics to professional communication. Hindi classes can be explained in Tamil or English, and English classes can be explained in Tamil or Hindi, depending on the learner’s needs. Our aim is to make learning practical, supportive and confidence-building through live online classes, guided practice and clear learning stages.
